How CarbonOS Works

A transparent journey from clean-energy data to carbon value

Every step is structured to improve traceability, reduce double counting, and make carbon-credit trading easier to understand.

01

Seller Claims Project

The seller selects a verified producer record and submits project details, energy generation, location, and reporting period.

02

Emissions Are Calculated

CarbonOS estimates avoided emissions using energy data, grid-emission factors, leakage deductions, and uncertainty adjustments.

03

Evidence Is Audited

Supporting records are reviewed before the project can proceed to approval and credit issuance.

04

Credits Are Approved

Approved carbon credits receive unique IDs, project references, vintage details, and verification status.

05

Buyers Purchase Credits

Buyers compare verified listings, prices, project categories, locations, and available quantities.

06

Credits Are Retired

Purchased credits can be permanently retired, creating a traceable record of the claimed climate impact.
