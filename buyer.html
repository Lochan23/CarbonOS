Retirement Process

How retirement works

1. Select owned credits Portfolio

Choose an active carbon-credit asset from your portfolio.

2. Enter retirement quantity Quantity

Enter how many credits you want to permanently retire.

3. Add retirement purpose Reason

Record the organization, campaign, event, or climate claim linked to the retirement.

4. Generate certificate Certificate

A permanent retirement certificate is generated with a unique verification reference.
